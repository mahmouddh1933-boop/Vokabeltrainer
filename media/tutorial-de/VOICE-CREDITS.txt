German narration: synthetic speech generated locally with Piper TTS, using the
Thorsten medium German voice model. This is not a recording of Mahmoud Barakat.

Voice model: de_DE-thorsten-medium
Sample rate: 22,050 Hz, resampled to 48,000 Hz for the MP4.
Model source: https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/download/tts-models/vits-piper-de_DE-thorsten-medium.tar.bz2
Original voice dataset: https://github.com/thorstenMueller/Thorsten-Voice
Dataset license stated in MODEL_CARD: CC0.
Piper implementation: https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl

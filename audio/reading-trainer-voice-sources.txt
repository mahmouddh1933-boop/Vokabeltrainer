حروف من الزيتون · مصادر مدرّب القراءة، الإصدار الثالث

Provider: Google Translate TTS, Arabic, normal-rate synthesis inside a spoken
carrier. Only the requested target after the sentence pause is included in the
learning audio. Input words and fragments are fully vocalized Arabic text.

Generation: tools/generate-reading-cloud-cache.py
Compilation: tools/build-reading-trainer.py --recordings /path/to/report.json
Provenance: audio/reading-trainer-manifest.json records the source commit, source
report checksum and checksum of each source recording and final compiled track.
Intermediate recordings are retained on the audio/reading-voice-recovery branch.

Application sentences: 300 complete vocalized sentences, synthesized at normal
rate with the same Google Arabic service. Sources, durations and hashes are in
audio/reading-sentences-manifest.json. No carrier extraction or word-level timing
is used for these sentence files. The target word is statically emphasized.

The app ships compiled synthetic recordings. No model weights or Piper engine
are shipped or used for this revision. IPA fields are pronunciation references;
they are not a claim that a speech engine faithfully realized each phoneme.

File decoding, signal checks and browser tests do not establish pronunciation
quality. Independent automatic speech recognition is used as an additional
check, with its limitations documented in docs/READING-TRAINER.md. It does not
replace a native Arabic speaker's listening assessment.

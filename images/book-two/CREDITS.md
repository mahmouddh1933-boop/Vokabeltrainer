# Workbook illustrations

76 SVG icons in `twemoji/` are from **Twemoji v14.0.2** by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, used under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2/assets/svg

The filenames identify the original Unicode emoji artwork. Original assets were downloaded over verified HTTPS from the versioned jsDelivr mirror. They are distributed without modification.

`special-words.png` contains six newly generated illustrations for shadow, fingernail, crow, branch, almonds and pumpkin. Panel order: left to right across the top row, then the bottom row. The pomegranate picture reuses `../letters/10.png`.

Workbook texts and exercises are newly written for this application. The supplied book photos informed the learning activities and do not appear in this distribution.

# العربية للشباب · Sources

The 59 SVG illustrations in `twemoji/` are unmodified **Twemoji v14.0.2**, by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original versioned source](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji/tree/v14.0.2/assets/svg). Downloaded using verified HTTPS from jsDelivr, or copied from the same version already bundled with the second-level workbook. Some images are symbolic cues for abstract vocabulary (for example a tree for a word root).

`carpet.png` is a newly generated transparent illustration of a woven carpet, used instead of a ball-of-yarn symbol for سَجَّادَةٌ.

`scenes.png` is a newly generated illustration sheet: four columns and two rows, read left to right, first the upper row, then the lower row. It depicts a station, dictionary study, hotel reception, day planning, road safety, a craft market, friends in a restaurant and a visit to Beijing. The illustrations are thematic; they are not exact portraits of dialogue characters or photographs from the book.

Unit/dialogue, reading and free-reading titles follow the contents photograph supplied by the user. All lesson texts, dialogues, definitions and exercises were written for this application, following the activity structure shown in the first unit. They are not a transcription of unprovided book pages. Unit 2 has no free-reading title in the supplied contents: “كلمة فتحت بابًا” is clearly labelled as an additional app reading.

The 221 Arabic audio files are synthetic speech, not a human recording. Inputs and source information: `../../audio/youth-manifest.json`. All are bundled locally and require no online speech service during playback. Automated checks verify decoding, duration and non-silent output; they do not constitute human phonetic review.

# تدريبات الشباب 2 · Illustrations and source material

`anatomy.png` is a newly generated, simplified front-view torso illustration for learning eight Arabic organ names. The numbered interactive markers are application annotations. Right and left refer to the depicted person's body (patient orientation), not the viewer. It supports vocabulary learning and is not a medical reference.

Other task pictures reuse the locally bundled Twemoji v14.0.2 illustrations (Twitter, Inc. and other contributors, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)) and the generated carpet illustration. Original credits: [Youth book](../youth/CREDITS.md), [Second-level workbook](../book-two/CREDITS.md). Some product/tool pictures are explicitly presented as symbolic writing prompts.

The 31 uploaded photographs were reviewed as teaching references. All 53 activities and 402 questions/tasks are newly authored analogues of their exercise forms, not photo reproductions. The original photographs are not distributed. [Photo-by-photo coverage](../../docs/YOUTH-TWO-COVERAGE.md) lists the exact supplied filenames, visible book pages and corresponding activities; a cropped/absent page number is left unknown.

Six listening words reuse previously bundled synthetic Arabic audio, as recorded in the existing audio manifests. New exercises do not download data or voice clips at runtime. Listening starts only after a user tap.
